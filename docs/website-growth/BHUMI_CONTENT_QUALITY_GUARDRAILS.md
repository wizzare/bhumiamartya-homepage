## 1. Rationale & Ethical Philosophy

Bhumi Amartya operates at the intersection of modern psychological inquiry, ancestral cultural wisdom (Nusantara, Vedic, Taoist), and contemporary metaphysical frameworks (Human Design, Astrologi, Destiny Matrix). 

This domain demands extraordinary rigor:
1. **Protecting Vulnerable Seekers**: Readers exploring self-discovery may be navigating profound life transitions, heartbreak, or existential distress. Content must empower, never exploit or induce fatalistic dread.
2. **Safeguarding Search & AI Authority**: Generative engines (Google, Perplexity, OpenAI) enforce strict YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) standards. Unsubstantiated medical claims or predatory promises will trigger severe domain penalties and algorithmic demotion.
3. **Preserving Bhumi's Authentic Voice**: While maintaining epistemic discipline, **we must never strip Bhumi of its poetic, contemplative, and sacred spiritual resonance.** The objective is clarity of context, not sterile skepticism.

## 3. Strict Domain Guardrails & Prohibitions

### 3.1 Medical, Psychiatric, & Mental Health Claims
- **Strict Prohibition**: Content must **NEVER** diagnose, treat, or claim to cure clinical psychiatric disorders (e.g., Clinical Depression, Bipolar Disorder, Schizophrenia, PTSD) using metaphysical systems.
- **Forbidden**: "Cakra ajna yang tersumbat adalah penyebab depresi klinis Anda."
- **Permitted & Encouraged**: "Dalam pemetaan energi tubuh, ketegangan pada area kepala sering kali mencerminkan kelelahan mental akibat overthinking. Praktik grounding dan meditasi napas dapat membantu menenangkan sistem saraf yang tertekan."
- **Mandatory Crisis Disclaimer**: Any article discussing deep grief, trauma, or emotional crisis must include a professional medical resource callout:
  > *Catatan Penting: Tulisan ini bersifat reflektif dan bukan pengganti konsultasi medis atau psikologis profesional. Jika Anda mengalami krisis emosional atau membutuhkan pendampingan klinis, silakan hubungi psikolog atau psikiater terdekat.*

### 3.2 Financial & Investment Prohibitions
- **Strict Prohibition**: Never promise guaranteed financial wealth, lottery wins, or investment success based on birth charts, numerology, or BaZi elements.
- **Forbidden**: "Dengan mengaktifkan garis uang 15 dalam Destiny Matrix, rezeki miliaran rupiah akan otomatis mengalir."
- **Permitted & Encouraged**: "Garis finansial dalam Destiny Matrix menggambarkan kecenderungan cara Anda mengelola nilai diri dan keterampilan profesional. Kesadaran akan pola ini membantu Anda membangun etos kerja yang lebih terarah."

### 3.3 Relationship Fatalism & Determinism
- **Strict Prohibition**: Never assert that a relationship is doomed to divorce, violence, or catastrophe because of incompatible weton or astrological synastry.
- **Forbidden**: "Pasangan dengan weton Wage dan Pahing dilarang menikah karena pasti bercerai."
- **Permitted & Encouraged**: "Dalam tradisi Primbon Jawa, perbedaan neptu tertentu diinterpretasikan sebagai sinyal perlunya kehati-hatian ekstra dalam komunikasi keluarga. Kearifan leluhur ini sejatinya mengajak pasangan untuk lebih sadar dalam mengelola gesekan, bukan membatalkan komitmen."

### 3.4 Astronomy vs. Astrology Distinction
- Scientific astronomical mechanics (e.g., planetary orbits, light-years, physics) must not be confused with astrological symbolism.
- Retrogrades must be framed as optical illusions from Earth's orbital perspective that serve as rich psychological metaphors for inward reflection.

## 4. AI Hallucination Prevention & Source Verification

When generative AI models draft content briefs or articles:

1. **Canonical Source Adherence**:
   - Human Design concepts must align with the original source literature (*Ra Uru Hu, Jovian Archive*).
   - Weton calculations must adhere to classical Javanese standards (*Kitab Primbon Betaljemur Adammakna*).
   - MBTI concepts must reference Carl Jung's *Psychological Types* and Myers-Briggs cognitive functions.
2. **No Fictitious Authorities or Citations**:
   - AI is strictly prohibited from inventing scientific studies, university research papers, or fictional doctors to justify spiritual claims.
3. **Epistemic Framing Language**:
   - Sentences must frequently employ clarifying qualifiers: *"Dalam tradisi astrologi...", "Secara simbolis...", "Dari kacamata reflektif...", "Sebagai alat pemetaan diri..."*.
